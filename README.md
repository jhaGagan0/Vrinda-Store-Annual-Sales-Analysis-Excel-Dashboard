# Vrinda Store Annual Sales Analysis | Excel Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing **Vrinda Store's 2022 sales data using Microsoft Excel** to understand customer purchasing behavior, evaluate sales performance, and identify growth opportunities for 2023.

The project follows an end-to-end data analytics workflow, including data cleaning, data processing, exploratory analysis, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, and an interactive dashboard. The final dashboard enables stakeholders to explore sales trends and make data-driven business decisions.

## 🎯 Business Problem

Vrinda Store sells products through multiple online channels and needs to understand its existing customer base and sales performance to improve its business strategy.

The key objectives were to:

- Analyze monthly sales trends and order performance.
- Understand purchasing behavior across genders and age groups.
- Identify top-performing states and sales channels.
- Evaluate product category performance.
- Recommend strategies to improve customer engagement and sales in 2023.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Excel** — Data analysis and dashboard development
- **Excel Formulas** — `IF`, `TEXT`, and other data-processing formulas
- **Pivot Tables** — Data aggregation and multidimensional analysis
- **Pivot Charts** — Visual representation of business metrics
- **Slicers** — Interactive filtering and dashboard exploration

## 🔄 Project Workflow

### 1. Data Cleaning

Prepared the raw dataset to improve data quality and consistency.

- Checked and handled missing or null values.
- Standardized gender labels, such as `M` and `W`, into `Man` and `Woman`.
- Corrected numerical formatting and ensured consistent data types.
- Improved dataset reliability for subsequent analysis.

### 2. Data Processing

Created additional fields to support meaningful analysis.

- **Age Group:** Used `IF` formulas to categorize customers into age groups.
- **Month:** Used the `TEXT` formula to extract month information from dates.
- Prepared the dataset for customer segmentation and time-based analysis.

### 3. Data Analysis

Created Pivot Tables to investigate key business questions.

- How do monthly sales and order volumes vary?
- Which gender contributes more to sales?
- Which age groups purchase the most?
- Which states generate the highest sales?
- Which online channels contribute significantly to overall sales?
- How does purchasing behavior vary across customer segments?

### 4. Data Visualization

Developed Pivot Charts to communicate findings clearly.

- Monthly sales trend analysis
- Gender-wise sales distribution
- Age-group purchasing analysis
- State-wise sales performance
- Sales channel comparison
- Product category analysis

### 5. Interactive Dashboard

Built an interactive Excel dashboard using Pivot Charts and Slicers.

Users can filter the dashboard by:

- Month
- Sales Channel
- Product Category

The interactive interface makes it easier to explore different customer segments and identify performance patterns.

## 📊 Key Business Insights

### 1. Customer Demographics
Women emerged as the primary customer segment, with adults aged **30–49 years** representing an important target audience.

### 2. Monthly Sales Performance
March recorded the highest sales volume among the months analyzed, indicating a potential seasonal or campaign-driven opportunity worth investigating.

### 3. Sales Channel Performance
Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra were significant sales channels, making them important platforms to evaluate for future marketing and promotional campaigns.

### 4. Geographic Performance
Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh were identified as key states for targeted customer acquisition and retention strategies.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actions could help improve sales performance:

- **Targeted Marketing:** Develop campaigns for women aged 30–49 using customer-specific offers.
- **Regional Promotions:** Focus marketing efforts on Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh.
- **Channel Optimization:** Evaluate campaign performance across Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra to improve channel-level returns.
- **Seasonal Campaigns:** Investigate the factors behind March's sales peak and replicate successful promotions where appropriate.
- **Customer Retention:** Use coupons and personalized offers to encourage repeat purchases.
- **Continuous Monitoring:** Track monthly performance and customer segments through the interactive dashboard.
